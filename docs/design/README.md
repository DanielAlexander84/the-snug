## The five vows

1. Does a tired, ashamed, overwhelmed person feel lifted and met, or judged?
2. Is the reward a real thing in the real world, or a shiny thing in here?
3. Does this welcome someone back without guilt?
4. Does it say "we", never "you, compared to them"?
5. Would we be glad if someone needed us less because of it?

## Parts of the mockups that are not the plan

- **"Give & get" balance bar and "Your standing":** dropped (D17).
- **Everything else not in a spec** (scheduled sessions such as "Tax Tuesday", "Coming back", mics, the two layouts, "lanterns lit today", Sage posting in the room feed and witnessing steps): ideas only. Nothing is built or decided until it has a spec in `docs/specs/`.
